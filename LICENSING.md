# Licensing

This repository contains two kinds of material under two licenses.

| Material | Folders | License |
|---|---|---|
| Hardware design: CAD, STL, drawings, renders, documentation | `cad/`, `stl/`, `docs/`, `images/` | [CERN Open Hardware Licence v2, Strongly Reciprocal (CERN-OHL-S-2.0)](LICENSE) |
| Software: analysis and Fusion 360 scripts | `analysis/`, `fusion/`, `tools/` | [MIT](LICENSE-MIT) |

CERN-OHL-S lets you build, modify, sell and share the design, provided that modified designs you distribute are released under the same licence, with their source files.

Source location for CERN-OHL-S purposes: https://github.com/caioplcerda/agriwing-100

Third-party CAD used for fit checks during design (Raspberry Pi 5, Camera Module 3, servo and cell models) is **not** included; the assembly STEP contains only this project's own parts.
